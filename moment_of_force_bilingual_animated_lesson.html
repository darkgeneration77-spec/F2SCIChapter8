The Moment of Force 力矩

What do we learn? 学什么？

  • A force can rotate an object at a fixed point.
    力可以使物体绕固定点转动。
  • The turning effect is called the moment of force.
    这种转动效应称为力矩。
  • Moment depends on force and perpendicular distance.
    力矩取决于力和垂直距离。
  • Moment can be clockwise or anticlockwise.
    力矩可分为顺时针和逆时针。
  • A balanced lever has equal moments on both sides.
    平衡杠杆两边的力矩相等。
口诀一：力矩 = 力 × 垂直距离
Moment = Force × Perpendicular distance
口诀二：同样的力，距离越大，力矩越大。
Same force, greater distance, greater moment.
口诀三：平衡时，顺时针力矩 = 逆时针力矩。
When balanced, clockwise moment = anticlockwise moment.

Principle of Moments 力矩原理

Load × Distance of load from fulcrum = Effort × Distance of effort from fulcrum
