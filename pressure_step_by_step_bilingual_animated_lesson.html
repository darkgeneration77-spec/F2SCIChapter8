Page 1 — Meaning and Formula 压强的概念与公式

What do we learn? 学什么？

Pressure is the effect of force acting on a surface.
压强是力作用在表面所产生的效果。

The effect depends on surface area.
压强的效果取决于接触面积。

Smaller surface area → greater pressure.
接触面积越小，压强越大。

Larger surface area → smaller pressure.
接触面积越大，压强越小。

口诀：
同样的力，面积小，压强大；面积大，压强小。
Same force: small area, high pressure; large area, low pressure.
Pressure = Force ÷ Surface area
压强 = 力 ÷ 表面积

Page 2 — Experiment 8.2 实验8.2

What do we learn? 学什么？

Aim: To study the relationship between surface area and pressure.
目的：研究表面积和压强之间的关系。

Problem statement: What is the effect of surface area on pressure produced by the same force?
问题陈述：在相同的力下，表面积对所产生压强有什么影响？

Hypothesis: The larger the surface area, the lower the pressure exerted.
假设：表面积越大，所施加的压强越小。

Constant variable: Metal blocks of the same mass.
固定变量：质量相同的金属块。

Manipulated variable: Surface area.
操纵变量：表面积。

Responding variable: Depth of dent.
反应变量：凹痕深度。

实验口诀：
改面积，看凹痕；质量相同才公平。
Change the area, observe the dent; keep the mass the same for a fair test.

Page 3 — Applications in Daily Life 日常生活中的应用

What do we learn? 学什么？

Skating boots have thin metal blades.
溜冰鞋具有薄金属刀片。

An axe has a thin blade.
斧头具有薄刀刃。

Tractors have big and wide wheels.
拖拉机具有大而宽的轮胎。

Football boots have studs.
足球鞋底有鞋钉。

应用口诀：
要切入：面积小；要不下陷：面积大。
To cut or penetrate: small area. To avoid sinking: large area.
