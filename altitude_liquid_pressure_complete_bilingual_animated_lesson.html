Page 1 — Altitude and Atmospheric Pressure

What do we learn? 学什么？

Atmospheric pressure decreases as altitude increases.
海拔越高，大气压力越低。

Air molecules are closer together near the Earth's surface because of gravitational attraction.
由于地心引力，靠近地面的空气粒子排列得更密。

At higher altitude, air molecules are less affected by gravity and are farther apart.
海拔越高，空气粒子受重力影响较小，排列得更疏。

Therefore, there are fewer air molecules pressing down at high altitude.
因此，高海拔处向下施压的空气粒子较少。

The balloon expands easily at high altitude because the atmospheric pressure is lower.
高海拔处大气压力较低，因此气球更容易膨胀。

口诀：
海拔高，空气少，向下压得少，气压低。
Higher altitude, fewer air molecules, less downward pressure, lower atmospheric pressure.

Page 2 — Effects of Depth on Liquid Pressure

Depth and Pressure 深度与压强

As depth increases, liquid pressure increases.
深度越大，液体压强越大。

The water above a deeper point is greater, so the force acting on that point is greater.
越深的位置，上方水量越多，因此作用在该点的力越大。

A diver experiences greater pressure when diving deeper.
潜水员潜得越深，所承受的压强越大。

Activity 8.11

Aim: To study the effect of depth on pressure in liquids.
目的：研究深度对液体压强的影响。

Air is pumped into the rubber tube. Air bubbles rise from the bottom to the surface.
把空气泵入橡胶管，气泡从底部上升到水面。

Observation: The air bubbles become larger as they rise.
观察：气泡上升时逐渐变大。

Reason: Liquid pressure decreases as depth decreases.
原因：深度减小时，液体压强减小。

口诀：
水越深，压越大；泡越上，压越小，泡越大。
Deeper water, higher pressure; rising bubbles, lower pressure, larger bubbles.

Page 3 — Applications of Depth and Liquid Pressure

Dam 大坝

The walls of a dam are thicker at the base because liquid pressure is greater at greater depth.
大坝底部的墙较厚，因为越深处液体压强越大。

Diver 潜水员

A diver wears a special suit to withstand high water pressure.
潜水员穿特殊潜水服，以承受较高水压。

Submarine 潜水艇

The body of a submarine is made of strong material so that it will not be crushed by high water pressure.
潜水艇由坚固材料制成，以免被高水压压坏。

应用口诀：
越深压力越大，所以底部要更强。
The deeper the water, the greater the pressure; therefore, the lower part must be stronger.

Page 4 — STEM and Formative Practice 8.2

STEM Experiment STEM实验

Water from the bottom hole shoots out farther than water from the upper hole.
下方小孔喷出的水比上方小孔喷得更远。

This is because liquid pressure is greater at greater depth.
这是因为越深处液体压强越大。

Depth for liquid pressure; area for pressure; moments for balance.
