1. What is Heat?

Heat（热） is a form of energy（能量）.

Unit of heat: joule (J)

Heat flows from a hotter region（较热区域） to a colder region（较冷区域）.

Absorb heat（吸热） → Temperature ↑   |   Release heat（放热） → Temperature ↓

2. What is Temperature?

Temperature（温度） is the degree of hotness or coldness（冷热程度） of an object.

Instrument: thermometer（温度计）

Units: °C / K

Faster particle movement（粒子运动） → higher temperature. Slower particle movement → lower temperature.

3. Heat ≠ Temperature

HEAT

Form of energy

Unit: J

TEMPERATURE

Degree of hotness or coldness

Unit: °C / K

Same temperature ≠ Same amount of heat

4. What affects the amount of heat?

TYPE

Type of material（材料种类）

QUANTITY

Quantity of material（材料数量）

TEMPERATURE

Temperature（温度）

AMOUNT OF HEAT → TYPE + QUANTITY + TEMPERATURE

5. Exam Application

100 mL water at 50°C and 300 mL water at 50°C have the same temperature, but not necessarily the same amount of heat（热量）.

Do not compare heat using temperature alone.

Compare Heat and Temperature

Heat is a form of energy and its unit is joule (J), whereas temperature is the degree of hotness or coldness of an object and its unit is °C or K.

HEAT → ENERGY → J

TEMPERATURE → HOT/COLD → °C/K

Absorb / Release

The object releases heat, causing its temperature to decrease.

ABSORB → TEMP ↑   |   RELEASE → TEMP ↓
