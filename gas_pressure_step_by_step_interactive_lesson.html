Page 1 — Gas Pressure and Activity 8.8

What do we learn? 学什么？

Air molecules are always moving.
空气粒子不断运动。

They collide with the walls of the container.
空气粒子不断撞击容器壁。

The collisions produce air pressure.
这些碰撞产生气压。

More frequent collisions produce greater pressure.
碰撞越频密，压强越大。

口诀：
粒子不停动，撞壁就有压。
Particles keep moving; collisions produce pressure.

Page 2 — Activity 8.9: Volume and Temperature

Part A — Volume 体积

When volume decreases, gas pressure increases.
体积减小时，气体压强增加。

When volume increases, gas pressure decreases.
体积增加时，气体压强减小。

口诀：
体积小，碰撞多，压强高；体积大，碰撞少，压强低。
Smaller volume, more collisions, higher pressure.

Part B — Temperature 温度

When temperature increases, particles move faster.
温度升高时，粒子移动得更快。

They collide with the container walls more frequently.
它们更频密地撞击容器壁。

Gas pressure increases.
气体压强增加。

口诀：
温度高，粒子快，碰撞多，压强高。
Higher temperature, faster particles, more collisions, higher pressure.
