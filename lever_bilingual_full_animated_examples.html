Chapter 8: Force and Motion — Lever

Lever 杠杆

What do we learn? 学什么？

  • Identify a lever.
    辨认什么是杠杆。
  • Identify the parts of a lever: effort, load, fulcrum and bar.
    辨认杠杆的部分：动力、负载、支点和杆。
  • State the purposes of a lever.
    说出杠杆的用途。
  • Classify levers into first, second and third class levers.
    把杠杆分类为第一类、第二类和第三类杠杆。
  • Label the position of load, effort and fulcrum.
    标示负载、动力和支点的位置。
口诀 1：
杠杆绕支点转；动力推，负载动。
A lever turns around the fulcrum; effort moves the load.
口诀 2：
一类支点中，二类负载中，三类动力中。
First: fulcrum in the middle.
Second: load in the middle.
Third: effort in the middle.

Activity 8.5

Aim: To discuss the various examples of levers in daily life according to their classes.

目的：根据杠杆的类别，讨论日常生活中各种杠杆的例子。

  1. Work in groups.
    分组进行。
  2. Every group has to find information regarding tools used in daily life and classify the tools into three classes of levers.
    每组寻找日常生活中所使用工具的资料，并把工具分类为三类杠杆。
  3. Label the position of load, effort and fulcrum on every tool.
    在每个工具上标示负载、动力和支点的位置。
  4. Present your group discussion in class.
    在课堂上呈现小组讨论结果。
