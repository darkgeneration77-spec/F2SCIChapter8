Page 1 — Factors that Affect Air Pressure

Volume 体积

When a closed container is compressed, the volume decreases.
当密闭容器被压缩时，体积减小。

The same number of air particles is now confined in a smaller space.
相同数量的空气粒子被限制在更小的空间内。

The particles collide more frequently with the container walls.
粒子更频密地撞击容器壁。

Therefore, air pressure increases.
因此，气压增加。

口诀：
体积小，粒子密，碰撞多，压强高。
Smaller volume, closer particles, more collisions, higher pressure.

Temperature 温度

When temperature increases, air particles move faster.
温度升高时，空气粒子移动得更快。

They collide with the walls more frequently and with greater force.
它们更频密并以更大的力撞击容器壁。

Therefore, air pressure increases.
因此，气压增加。

口诀：
温度高，粒子快，撞得多又强，压强高。
Higher temperature, faster particles, more frequent and stronger collisions, higher pressure.

Page 2 — Atmospheric Pressure and Activity 8.10

Atmospheric Pressure 大气压力

When air inside a packet is sucked out, the packet is compressed.
当包装袋里的空气被吸出时，包装袋会被压扁。

The outside air pressure is higher than the pressure inside the packet.
袋外的大气压力高于袋内的气压。

The higher outside pressure pushes the packet inward.
较高的外部气压把包装袋向内压缩。

Atmospheric pressure is the pressure exerted by the atmosphere on Earth and all objects on Earth.
大气压力是大气层对地球及地球上所有物体所施加的压力。

口诀：
里面压低，外面压高，外压把物体向内推。
Lower pressure inside, higher pressure outside; outside pressure pushes inward.

Page 3 — Activity 8.10: Six Demonstrations

1. Plunger 吸盘

Pressing the plunger forces air out and creates low pressure inside.
按下吸盘会把空气挤出，使内部形成低压。

Higher atmospheric pressure outside presses the plunger tightly against the tile.
较高的外部大气压力把吸盘紧压在瓷砖上。

2. Magdeburg Hemispheres 马德堡半球

Air is removed from between the hemispheres, producing a vacuum.
两半球之间的空气被抽走，形成真空。

Atmospheric pressure outside presses the hemispheres together, making them difficult to pull apart.
外部大气压力把两个半球压在一起，因此很难拉开。

3. Straw 吸管

Sucking lowers the pressure inside the straw.
吸吮使吸管内的气压降低。

Atmospheric pressure on the drink pushes the liquid up the straw and into the mouth.
饮料表面的大气压力把液体推上吸管并进入口中。

4. Siphon 虹吸管

The end of tube B is lower than the end of tube A, causing water to flow out from B.
B端低于A端，使水从B端流出。

Water in the tube flows out and creates a lower pressure in the tube.
管内水流出并形成较低气压。

Atmospheric pressure pushes water into the tube, so water flows continuously.
大气压力把水推入管内，因此水持续流动。

5. Syringe 针筒

Pulling the piston increases the volume of air in the cylinder.
拉动活塞会增加针筒内空气的体积。

This lowers the air pressure inside the syringe.
这使针筒内的气压降低。

Higher atmospheric pressure outside pushes water into the syringe.
较高的外部大气压力把水推入针筒。

6. Vacuum Cleaner 吸尘器

The fan forces air out of the vacuum cleaner and produces low pressure inside.
风扇把空气排出吸尘器，使内部形成低压。

Higher atmospheric pressure outside pushes air and dust into the vacuum cleaner.
较高的外部大气压力把空气和灰尘推入吸尘器。

Page 4 — Applications of Air Pressure in Daily Life

Plunger 吸盘

Pressing the plunger forces air out and creates low pressure inside.
按下吸盘排出空气，使内部形成低压。

High pressure in the pipe pushes out the blockage when the plunger is pushed.
压下吸盘时，管内较高压力把堵塞物推出。

When the plunger is pulled up, pressure inside is reduced and higher outside pressure pushes water and blockage upward.
拉起吸盘时，内部压力降低，较高的外部气压把水和堵塞物向上推。

Magdeburg Hemisphere 马德堡半球

Pumping air out creates a vacuum inside.
抽出空气使内部形成真空。

Atmospheric pressure outside presses the hemispheres together and creates an airtight seal.
外部大气压力把半球压紧并形成气密密封。

Straw 吸管

Sucking lowers the pressure inside the straw.
吸吮降低吸管内的气压。

Atmospheric pressure pushes the liquid up the straw.
大气压力把液体推上吸管。

Siphon 虹吸管

Tube end B is lower than tube end A, so water flows out through B.
B端低于A端，所以水从B端流出。

Atmospheric pressure pushes water into the tube and maintains continuous flow.
大气压力把水推入管中并维持连续水流。

总口诀：
先造低压，再由高压来推动。
First create a low-pressure region; then higher atmospheric pressure does the pushing.

Page 5 — Syringe, Vacuum Cleaner and Condensed Milk Can

Syringe 针筒

Pulling the piston increases the volume of air in the cylinder.
拉动活塞会增加针筒内空气的体积。

The pressure inside the syringe decreases.
针筒内的气压降低。

Higher atmospheric pressure outside pushes the liquid into the syringe.
外部较高的大气压力把液体推入针筒。

Vacuum Cleaner 吸尘器

The fan forces air out and creates low pressure inside the vacuum cleaner.
风扇把空气排出，使吸尘器内部形成低压。

Higher atmospheric pressure outside pushes air and dust into the vacuum cleaner.
外部较高的大气压力把空气和灰尘推入吸尘器。

Condensed Milk Can 炼乳罐

With only one small hole, milk does not flow out easily because air cannot enter the can to replace the milk leaving it.
只有一个小孔时，空气不能进入罐内补充流出的炼乳，因此炼乳不容易流出。

With two holes, one hole allows air to enter while milk flows out through the other hole.
有两个孔时，一个孔让空气进入，另一个孔让炼乳流出。

Air pressure inside and outside becomes more balanced, so the milk flows smoothly.
罐内外气压更接近平衡，因此炼乳能顺畅流出。

口诀：
一个孔：气进不去，液体难流；两个孔：一进气，一出液。
One hole: air cannot enter, liquid flows poorly. Two holes: one for air in, one for liquid out.
