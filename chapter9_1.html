Page 1/5 · What is Heat?

Heat（热） is a form of energy（能量）.

Unit: joule (J)（焦耳）

Heat flows from a hotter region（较热区域） to a colder region（较冷区域）.

Absorb heat（吸收热量）

Temperature ↑

When an object absorbs heat, its temperature increases.

Release heat（释放热量）

Temperature ↓

When an object releases heat, its temperature decreases.

HEAT = ENERGY → J | HOT → COLD | ABSORB ↑ | RELEASE ↓

Page 2/5 · What is Temperature?

Temperature（温度） is the degree of hotness or coldness（冷热程度） of an object.

Instrument: thermometer（温度计）

Units: °C / K

Faster particles（粒子更快）

Particle movement ↑ → Temperature ↑

Slower particles（粒子更慢）

Particle movement ↓ → Temperature ↓

Exam Point

The sense of touch（触觉） is not accurate for measuring temperature. A thermometer gives a more accurate measurement.

TEMPERATURE = HOW HOT / COLD → THERMOMETER → °C / K

Page 3/5 · Heat vs Temperature

HEAT（热）

Form of energy（能量）

Unit = J

TEMPERATURE（温度）

Degree of hotness or coldness（冷热程度）

Unit = °C / K

100 mL vs 200 mL Water

100 mL water at 50°C and 200 mL water at 50°C have the same temperature, but they do not necessarily have the same amount of heat（热量）.

Same temperature ≠ Same amount of heat

Higher temperature does not always mean a greater amount of heat.

HEAT ≠ TEMPERATURE

Page 4/5 · What Affects the Amount of Heat?

The amount of heat（热量） depends on THREE factors:

TYPE

Type of material（材料种类）

QUANTITY

Quantity of material（材料数量）

TEMPERATURE

Temperature（温度）

Fair Comparison

Do not compare heat using temperature alone. Check the type, quantity and temperature.

AMOUNT OF HEAT → TYPE + QUANTITY + TEMPERATURE
