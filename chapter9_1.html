Complete Notes

Heat（热） is a form of energy（能量）. Its unit is joule (J). Heat flows from a hotter region to a colder region. Absorbing heat increases temperature; releasing heat decreases temperature.

Temperature（温度） is the degree of hotness or coldness（冷热程度） of an object. It is measured using a thermometer（温度计） in °C or K. Faster particle movement means higher temperature.

The amount of heat（热量） depends on type of material（材料种类） + quantity of material（材料数量） + temperature（温度）.

HEAT → ENERGY → J | TEMP → HOT/COLD → °C/K | HEAT AMOUNT → TYPE + QUANTITY + TEMP
