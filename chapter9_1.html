Complete Notes

Heat（热） is a form of energy（能量）. Its unit is joule (J). Heat flows from a hotter region to a colder region. When an object absorbs heat, its temperature increases. When it releases heat, its temperature decreases.

Temperature（温度） is the degree of hotness or coldness（冷热程度） of an object. It is measured using a thermometer（温度计） in °C or K. Faster particle movement means higher temperature; slower particle movement means lower temperature.

The amount of heat（热量） depends on type of material（材料种类） + quantity of material（材料数量） + temperature（温度）. Do not compare heat using temperature alone.

HEAT → ENERGY → J | TEMPERATURE → HOT/COLD → °C/K | AMOUNT OF HEAT → TYPE + QUANTITY + TEMPERATURE
