Page 1/5 · What is Heat?

Quick Summary
Heat（热） is a form of energy（能量）. Unit = joule (J). Heat flows from a hotter region to a colder region.
HEAT → ENERGY → J · HOT → COLD

Page 2/5 · What is Temperature?

Quick Summary
Temperature（温度） tells how hot or cold an object is. It is measured using a thermometer（温度计） in °C or K. Faster particles → higher temperature.
TEMP → HOT / COLD → THERMOMETER → °C / K

Page 3/5 · Heat vs Temperature

Quick Summary
Heat and temperature are different. Heat = energy; temperature = degree of hotness or coldness. The same temperature does not mean the same amount of heat.
HEAT ≠ TEMPERATURE · SAME TEMP ≠ SAME HEAT AMOUNT

Page 4/5 · Amount of Heat

Quick Summary
The amount of heat（热量） depends on the type of material（材料种类）, quantity of material（材料数量） and temperature（温度）.
HEAT AMOUNT → TYPE + QUANTITY + TEMPERATURE

Page 5/5 · Exam Review

Quick Summary
Absorb heat → temperature increases. Release heat → temperature decreases. Use a thermometer for accurate temperature measurement. Never compare heat using temperature alone.
